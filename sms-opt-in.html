SMS text message opt-in & consent

This page documents how patients and callers explicitly consent to receive SMS (text) messages from participating clinics that use FrontDesk Intelligence. This consent is separate from our Privacy Policy or Terms of Service.

SMS consent statement

By calling a participating clinic’s published phone number from your mobile phone, you consent to receive SMS text messages from that clinic at the same phone number you called from, if your call is not answered by staff.

You agree to receive text messages about your inquiry, including missed-call follow-up texts, scheduling assistance, appointment-related questions, and staff replies in the same conversation thread. Message frequency varies based on your inquiry. Message and data rates may apply.

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP or contact the clinic directly. Consent to receive SMS messages is not required as a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

How SMS opt-in works (step by step)

Consent is obtained through a call-initiated opt-in flow. There is no web checkbox. The caller takes a clear action — placing a phone call — and then receives an SMS with full disclosure before any ongoing text conversation.

  1. Step 1 — Caller initiates contact. The patient or prospective patient dials the clinic’s advertised phone number (shown on the clinic website, Google Business Profile, signage, or other published materials).
  2. Step 2 — Call is not answered. The call is not completed with clinic staff (for example: no answer, busy, or a short call that ends without a substantive conversation).
  3. Step 3 — Clinic sends first SMS text message. Within approximately 30 seconds, the caller receives an automated SMS text message at the same mobile number they called from, sent from the clinic’s dedicated toll-free or messaging number.
  4. Step 4 — First SMS includes full SMS disclosure. The first text message identifies the clinic by name, states that the clinic missed their call, explains that the message is an SMS follow-up, describes what types of texts they may receive, and includes STOP/HELP opt-out language plus “Msg & data rates may apply.”
  5. Step 5 — Ongoing SMS only with engagement. Additional SMS text messages are sent only if the caller replies to that thread, clinic staff respond in the same thread, or a limited service reminder is sent for the same open inquiry. We do not send SMS to numbers that never called the clinic.

Exact SMS text shown to the user (first message)

This is the sample first SMS text message sent after a missed call. This language is shown directly to the recipient — it is not buried in our Privacy Policy or Terms of Service.

Sample first SMS text message Hi — we missed your call at [Clinic Name]. We would like to follow up by SMS text message to help with scheduling or questions about your inquiry. Reply here to continue by text. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

What SMS text messages you agree to receive

When you opt in by calling a participating clinic, you consent to receive the following types of SMS text messages at the phone number you called from:

  • Missed-call follow-up texts — an automated SMS sent shortly after your unanswered call
  • Scheduling and service texts — messages to help book appointments or answer questions about the service you inquired about
  • Staff reply texts — responses from clinic staff in the same SMS conversation thread
  • Service reminder texts — limited follow-up reminders tied to your open inquiry (not marketing broadcasts)

What SMS text messages you will NOT receive

  • Marketing or promotional text blasts unrelated to your call
  • Messages to phone numbers that never called the clinic
  • Messages from purchased or third-party contact lists

Message frequency & cost

  • Frequency: Typically one initial automated SMS per missed call, plus replies in the same conversation. Service reminders, if sent, are limited to the open inquiry.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier.
  • Opt-out: Reply STOP, STOPALL, UNSUBSCRIBE, CANCEL, END, or QUIT to stop receiving SMS messages.
  • Help: Reply HELP for clinic contact information and opt-out instructions.

SMS consent is separate from Privacy Policy & Terms

Consent to receive SMS text messages is obtained through the call-initiated flow and first-message disclosure described above. It is not obtained by accepting our website Privacy Policy or Terms of Service. Those documents govern data handling and platform use — not SMS opt-in.
