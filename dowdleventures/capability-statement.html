Company overview

Dowdle Ventures LLC is a family-run software company founded by brothers Trevor and Jakob Dowdle. We build practical software and AI tools for businesses, nonprofits and government agencies, especially teams that don't have engineers of their own. Clients work directly with the two engineers who build their software.

Core competencies

  • Custom web applications and internal tools
  • Integrations between existing systems and data sources
  • AI and automation for repetitive, high-volume work
  • Cloud infrastructure, hosting and deployment

Differentiators

  • Direct access to the engineers doing the work, with no account managers in between.
  • Cloud and application engineering experience: Trevor has worked in the software industry since 2011, including as a Senior Cloud Solutions Engineer; Jakob holds a computer science degree from Southern Utah University.
  • We build and run our own product, FrontDesk Intelligence, a text follow-up service for missed calls.
  • Remote team, working with organizations anywhere in the U.S.
