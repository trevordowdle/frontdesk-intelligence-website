SMS opt-in methodology

Public documentation of how patients consent to receive missed-call follow-up text messages from participating clinics that use FrontDesk Intelligence.

Overview

Use case: A patient calls a participating clinic’s published phone number. If the call is not answered by staff, the caller may receive an automated follow-up SMS to the same phone number they called from. The patient may then reply to continue the conversation by text.

This program is transactional customer care — not marketing broadcast messaging. We do not send messages to phone numbers that have not placed an inbound call to the clinic (except replies within an existing thread started by this flow).

Who receives messages

Patients and prospective patients who call a participating clinic’s advertised phone number from the phone number we text.

How consent is obtained

  1. The consumer initiates contact by dialing the clinic’s published phone number (website, Google Business Profile, signage, etc.).
  2. The call is not completed with staff per the clinic’s telephony setup (for example: no answer, busy, or a short call that ends without a substantive conversation).
  3. Shortly after that call (target: within approximately 30 seconds), the caller receives one automated SMS at the same number they called from, from the clinic’s dedicated messaging number.
  4. The first SMS includes:
    • Clinic name (identifies sender)
    • Statement that the clinic missed their call
    • Purpose: scheduling and questions related to their inquiry
    • Instruction to reply to continue by text
    • Opt-out: Reply STOP to unsubscribe; HELP for help; message and data rates may apply
  5. Further messages occur only when:
    • the consumer replies to that thread,
    • clinic staff respond in the same thread to assist the consumer, or
    • a configured service reminder tied to that open inquiry (for example, a patient nudge if no reply) — not broadcast marketing.
  6. Opt-out: Consumers may reply STOP (and other standard keywords) to end messages. Opt-out is honored promptly and persisted in our systems.

Sample first message

Hi — we missed your call at [Clinic Name]. Reply here and we'll help with scheduling or questions. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out.

Message types

Allowed Not allowed via this flow
Missed-call follow-up Cold outreach or purchased lists
Scheduling and service questions Unrelated promotional blasts
Staff replies in an existing thread Messages to numbers that never called

Why this is not a web checkbox opt-in

For this flow, opt-in is call-initiated contact plus clear disclosure in the first SMS. Separate written consent may still be required for marketing programs or certain jurisdictions.

One-sentence summary

Consumers opt in by calling the clinic’s published phone number; if the call is not answered, they receive a follow-up SMS to the calling number identifying the clinic, explaining we missed their call, and offering help by reply, with STOP to opt out.

Frequency

Message frequency varies based on the patient’s inquiry. Typically one initial automated message per missed call, plus replies in the same conversation thread. Service reminders, if enabled, are limited to the open inquiry.
