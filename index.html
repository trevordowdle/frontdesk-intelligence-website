What happens after a missed call

  1. A patient calls your clinic’s published phone number.
  2. If staff do not answer, our system detects the missed call.
  3. The caller receives a timely SMS from your clinic’s dedicated number.
  4. They can reply to share service interest, timing, and questions.
  5. Your team receives a qualified summary to follow up and book.

Built for clinics that cannot afford to lose inbound leads

Most practices still rely on voicemail and delayed callbacks. By then, callers may have moved on. We sit at the front of your workflow — before scheduling — and help you capture intent while interest is highest.

Instant missed-call recovery

Automated SMS follow-up to the calling number shortly after an unanswered call, identifying your clinic and inviting the patient to continue by text.

Lead qualification

Structured SMS conversations capture service interest, urgency, preferred times, and common questions so staff start with context, not a cold callback.

Staff notifications

Clean summaries and lead details routed to your team so front desk staff can convert inquiries faster without replacing your existing scheduling software.

How we work with your existing stack

FrontDesk Intelligence is not a phone system replacement or scheduling platform. We integrate alongside what you already use.

Keep your published number

Clinics can forward unanswered calls to a dedicated line we operate, or use a Twilio-hosted number — without forcing a full port on day one.

Transactional SMS only

Messages are limited to missed-call follow-up and related patient care conversations — not cold outreach or purchased lists. See our SMS opt-in methodology.

Compliance-first design

STOP/HELP handling, opt-out persistence, webhook security, and documented consent flows support carrier verification and responsible messaging.
